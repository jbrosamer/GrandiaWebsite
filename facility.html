The Property

Built for the Dressage Horse

Off Broadway Farm is a premier private dressage facility in Snohomish, WA, purpose-built to provide the ideal training and boarding environment for horses and riders at every level. From the custom stall hardware to the competition-quality arena footing, every element has been chosen with care.

Our knowledgeable staff is on-site daily to ensure each horse receives the individualized attention it deserves — feeding, turnout, blanketing, and basic health monitoring all tailored to the individual.

Amenities

Facility Amenities

Off Broadway Farm provides everything a dedicated dressage horse and rider need to train, compete, and thrive — year-round, in the Pacific Northwest.

Stabling

  • Spacious box stalls with custom hardwood doors and decorative metal grillwork
  • Individual turnout paddocks with quality footing
  • Individualized daily care and feeding programs
  • Hot and cold wash stalls

Arenas & Training

  • Full-size outdoor dressage arena with professional footing
  • Large indoor arena — competition-quality, all-weather footing
  • Covered arena for year-round riding regardless of weather
  • On-site training with a USDF Gold, Silver & Bronze Medalist

Rider Amenities

  • Elegant tack rooms with engraved personal lockers
  • Tiered saddle racks and organized bridle storage
  • Welcoming community of dedicated dressage riders
