Our Philosophy

Training Built on Trust & Positive Partnership

Grandia Dressage is led by Nicki Grandia alongside assistant trainer Abby Saar located at Off Broadway Farm in Snohomish, WA. Nicki is a USDF Gold, Silver, and Bronze Medal program. We offer customized training programs designed to fit the individual needs of horses and riders at every level — from Intro to the FEI.

Abby has been part of the team for nearly ten years, specializing in bringing young horses along and giving them the foundation and confidence they need to excel. With a strong Pony Club background, she is a vital part of our daily training program and farm management.
