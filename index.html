Our Philosophy

Training Built on Trust & Positive Partnership

Grandia Dressage is led by Nicki Grandia alongside assistant trainer Abby Saar located at Off Broadway Farm in Snohomish, WA. Nicki is a USDF Gold, Silver, and Bronze Medal program. We offer customized training programs designed to fit the individual needs of horses and riders at every level — from Intro to the FEI.

Abby has been part of the team for nearly ten years, specializing in bringing young horses along and giving them the foundation and confidence they need to excel. With a strong Pony Club background, she is a vital part of our daily training program and farm management.

Services

Training, Coaching & Boarding

In-Hand & Under Saddle

Training & Development

Full training rides for horses of all backgrounds and experience levels — from first miles under saddle to FEI work. Each program is progressive and systematic, built around where your horse is today, with special care given to setting young horses up with the confidence and foundation they need to excel.

Lessons, Clinics & Competition

Coaching & Lessons

From single lessons to intensive coaching packages and clinic weekends, we work with riders at every level and ambition. Whether you're preparing for your first schooling show or chasing regional scores, we help you arrive confident, focused, and ready — in person, haul-in, or virtually.
