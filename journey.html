Horse Syndicate

Own a Piece of the Journey

Grandia Dressage invites a small group of partners to share in the ownership, costs, and joy of a competition dressage horse — without the burden of full ownership.

Express Interest
Syndicate

Shared Ownership, Shared Pride

A horse syndicate brings together a small group of people who co-own a horse, share the annual costs, and share in everything that follows — the training milestones, the competition results, the quiet moments in the barn.

Your horse lives at Off Broadway Farm under the full-time care of the Grandia Dressage team. You get regular updates, barn access, and a front-row seat to the competition journey — without a single feed bill landing in your mailbox.

Structure

How It Works

Syndicates are intentionally kept small — typically four to ten shares — so every member has a real stake and a real voice.

01

Buy a Share

Members purchase an ownership percentage of the horse. The buy-in covers your share of the horse's value and sets the foundation of the partnership.

02

Share the Costs

Annual expenses — board, training, veterinary care, farrier, and competition fees — are divided among shareholders. Each member contributes proportionally, making serious competition accessible.

03

Follow the Journey

Regular updates, barn visits, competition invitations, and major decision-making are shared with the group. You are an owner in every meaningful sense.
